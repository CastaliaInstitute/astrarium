Interplanetary path screen

—

Choose spatial endpoints and a time direction. Backward mode is reverse playback of a forward-time modeled orbit; it does not solve continuous traveler motion toward a decreasing environment epoch.

Forward routes use a zero-revolution Lambert transfer and integrate under modeled gravity, then screen planets, covered moons, curated small bodies, available recorded spacecraft, and sampled satellite TLEs. Backward mode swaps endpoints, solves an ordinary forward-time arc, and reverses its playback. It does not integrate a forward-experiencing traveler whose environment epoch decreases continuously. Collision results apply only to the synthetic spatial arc and covered object records. Coverage gaps, unmodeled objects, and orbit uncertainty remain; no result certifies a time-travel route.

Stellar cartography

For a steadier solve, prop the phone securely on a surface facing up, with an unobstructed view of the sky. Keep it still while the app combines a short burst of frames, then matches persistent points against bright stars predicted from your location, time, and phone orientation.
