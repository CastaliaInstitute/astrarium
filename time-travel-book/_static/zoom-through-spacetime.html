Cosmic Zoom: redshift as a time label

What the chronal rate means

The selected remote scene-address changes by about 2.63 × 10⁸ remote seconds for each second of crew time. This is a demand on the fictional channel, not a photon locally travelling at that speed.

Science-fiction rule: the crew's body and clock stay with A.R.G.O. The chronal echo carries a changing information pattern; the dome and a fictional neural display turn it into a lived view. Quantum entanglement may help label or compare patterns, but known entanglement cannot send the picture or choose the past scene. The story's echo supplies that missing rule.

To make the imagined channel more than a pretty line, a theory must specify its field equation, energy and momentum source, noise and resolution, endpoint events, and causal rules. If it lets information reach the past, it must also explain how the resulting history remains self-consistent. Every point on the plotted line is a demand on that theory.
