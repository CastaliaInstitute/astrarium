Earth is not a date pinned to a map

Drag the slider to select a past Earth event. The plotted path combines JPL DE441 Earth–Sun states with an approximate local Galactic model for the Sun.

Frame matters. These coordinates are relative to Earth's present center in a local Galactic inertial approximation. At roughly 2,032 years of look-back, the modeled Earth center is about 1.64 light-years from its present position in this frame. That is not a distance an A.R.G.O. hull must fly: it is a comparison between two Earth events in one declared coordinate model.
