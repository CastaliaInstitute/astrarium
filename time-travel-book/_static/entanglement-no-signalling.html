Local result stays random

P(A = +1) = 1/2 for every remote setting. The remote choice does not encode a readable message in A.R.G.O.'s local results.

What this means for the mission: entanglement could be a shared “score” for comparing measurements, or a powerful ingredient in a quantum communication protocol. By itself it cannot send the Star's image, select a past event, or make the crew experience a distant viewpoint. It also does not reverse the order of events.

Singlet: |ψ⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ − |↓↑⟩)/√2. Local state: ρA = TrB(|ψ⟩⟨ψ|) = I/2, independent of what is measured at B.
