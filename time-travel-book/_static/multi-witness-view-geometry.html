Brightness assumes isotropic-equivalent point emission uniformly distributed over the selected cone, a flat spectrum in the chosen band, and no extinction. A view outside the cone or below Earth's geometric horizon is marked unseen.

Interpretation: a single physical source does not paint the same sky coordinate for everyone. Nearby witnesses see different elevations and parallax; witnesses outside the beam or below the horizon may see nothing. To claim that a pattern resembles a structured craft to several observers, A.R.G.O. must solve the source's orientation and wavefront, propagate the photons to each actual witness, and compare their separate angular tracks. The Star narrative has no measured observer positions or angular record to fit, so these sliders explore possibilities rather than recover a unique sighting.
