# A.R.G.O. as the Star of Bethlehem

**A.R.G.O. — Astrarium Research & Galactic Observatory**
The lead mission premise is that A.R.G.O. traveled back through space and time and became the Star of Bethlehem. This is the hypothesis the book explores, not an established historical claim. The interactive chapters compute conditional worldline estimates and compare them with natural-sky explanations.

This Jupyter Book combines the Astrarium whitepaper and model notes with executable equations and parameterized graphs. The calculations are deliberately separated by status: general-relativistic results from cited papers, flat-background kinematic illustrations, and product hypotheses are not interchangeable.

## What the book computes

1. **Two-bubble CTC kinematics.** It evaluates the published symmetric closure condition and a derived flat-background endpoint relation. The long-lookback example is a scale illustration; the endpoint extension does not retarget the paper's full compact-support metric or close a loop through Bethlehem.
2. **Oblate single-bubble density slice.** It evaluates an exact integral of Eulerian energy density for a separate prescribed unit-lapse profile. It is not a source calculation for the two-bubble CTC, total energy budget, ADM mass, or engineering design.
3. **Historical context and navigation constraints.** The Star of Bethlehem story is treated as an underconstrained observation narrative. A.R.G.O. as its source is a speculative hypothesis; natural-sky explanations, including Jupiter, remain explicit comparators.

## Status labels

- **Published geometry:** a mathematical spacetime and worldline result under its stated assumptions.
- **Kinematic illustration:** an equation-level estimate in a simplified frame model.
- **Unsolved extension:** a requirement not yet demonstrated in the cited geometry or current Astrarium implementation.
- **Speculative premise:** a product or narrative hypothesis, such as A.R.G.O. being the Star of Bethlehem.

The primary mission premise is that A.R.G.O. became the Star described in Matthew 2; this is a speculative hypothesis, not a claim about established history. The interactive figures are generated by executing the notebooks at build time. Their source cells remain visible and editable. All input values and generated plots are illustrative unless a cell explicitly identifies a source-backed datum.
