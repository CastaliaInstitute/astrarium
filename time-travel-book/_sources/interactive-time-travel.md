# Travelling Through Space In Time

**A.R.G.O. — Astrarium Research & Galactic Observatory**
The lead mission sends A.R.G.O. continuously back through space and time to become the Star of Bethlehem. The book adopts that identity as its mission premise, then asks what a continuous route, observer encounter, and physical drive model would require. The historical narrative does not independently establish that the Star was a vehicle.

This single Jupyter Book brings the mission narrative, temporal-drive model, executable calculations, Astrarium implementation, and observation/validation material into one reading path. Its central distinction is between a mission premise, a result derived inside a stated mathematical model, an Astrarium visualization, and demonstrated navigation capability.

## What this book establishes and computes

1. **Two-bubble CTC kinematics.** It evaluates the published symmetric closure condition and a derived flat-background endpoint relation. For the stated 6 BCE scale input, the extension gives a roughly 16,563-year first leg and 38,976-light-year separation. That is a warning about the chosen flat model's scale, not a mission route: it does not retarget the paper's full compact-support metric or close a loop through Bethlehem.
2. **Oblate single-bubble density slice.** It evaluates an exact integral of Eulerian energy density for a separate prescribed unit-lapse profile. It is not a source calculation for the two-bubble CTC, total energy budget, ADM mass, or engineering design.
3. **Historical context and navigation constraints.** A.R.G.O. is the Star in the mission scenario; the source narrative does not fix an exact date, position, range, or track. Natural-sky reconstructions, including Jupiter, are comparison cases, not replacements for the premise.

## Status labels

- **Published geometry:** a mathematical spacetime and worldline result under its stated assumptions.
- **Kinematic illustration:** an equation-level estimate in a simplified frame model.
- **Unsolved extension:** a requirement not yet demonstrated in the cited geometry or current Astrarium implementation.
- **Mission premise:** A.R.G.O. is the Star described in Matthew 2.
