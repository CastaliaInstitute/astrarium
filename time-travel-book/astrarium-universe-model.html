Astrarium Universe Model#

The universe of planetary systems is generated, not stored. Every Gaia source resolves to a deterministic planetary system via a pure function:

system(source_id, model_version, [G, parallax, BP-RP])

Same inputs → same planets, forever. Nothing is persisted; 1.8B stars thus carry billions of planets at zero storage cost.

Provenance states#

State

Meaning

observed

Real, confirmed exoplanet (NASA Exoplanet Archive PSCompPars, matched by Gaia source id)

candidate

Published candidate (pl_controv_flag)

inferred

Generated by the current occurrence model

procedural

Render-time detail only (moons, terrain, clouds) — never cataloged

Precedence: observed > candidate > inferred. When a real discovery lands in a future archive refresh, it replaces the synthetic planet at that slot.

The version string is salted into every seed, so switching models yields a different (but internally consistent) universe. Clients may preserve a previous model’s universe by pinning the version.

Seeds and determinism#

seed = blake2b("{model}:{source_id}:{salt}", 8 bytes)

All draws use a portable 64-bit LCG (s = s*6364136223846793005 + 1442695040888963407), identical across the Python (reference), Kotlin, and JS implementations. Golden vectors in scripts/planetforge.py pin the ports.

Occurrence model (AUM-1)#

  1. Stellar type from BP-RP color + parallax (Teff → class M/K/G/F/A/B).

  2. Small planets per star: Poisson(λ = 1.1 FGK, 0.7 M, 0.4 A/B) — Kepler’s finding that small planets are ubiquitous; giants are rare: P(giant) = {M 2%, K 5%, G 9%, F 11%, A 14%, B 20%}.

  3. Chain spacing: successive orbits multiply by 10^(0.28±0.08) AU (log-normal), anchored at the inner edge for the class, past the snow line for giants.

  4. Radii: small planets log-normal around ~1.3 R⊕ (small-planet prevalence); giants log-normal around ~10 R⊕.

  5. Eccentricity: Rayleigh-like, σ=0.03 compact, σ=0.15 giants.

  6. Equilibrium temperature: 278 K · L^0.25 / √a.

Rendered detail (terrain, moons, clouds) is procedural per visit and always labeled procedural.
